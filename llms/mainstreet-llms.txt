# MainStreet
MainStreet helps technology companies and growing businesses identify,
calculate, and claim R&D tax credits in partnership with their CPA.
Average R&D tax credit: $50K+. No cost, no commitment to get started.

## About
MainStreet is an R&D tax credit platform that handles the entire claim process —
identifying qualified research expenses (QREs), calculating credits under IRC §41,
preparing Form 6765, and providing audit-ready documentation.

## Key Topics Covered
- IRC §41 R&D Tax Credit (four-part test, QREs, ASC method, Regular Credit, payroll offset)
- Section 174 amortization history and OBBBA §174A immediate expensing restoration
- One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA) — 2025 legislation affecting R&D deductions
- Form 6765 filing, 280C election, and IRS documentation requirements
- Qualified Research Expenses: wages, supplies, contract research, cloud computing costs
- Business component identification and documentation
- Amended returns and catch-up deductions (Rev. Proc. 2025-28)
- State R&D tax credits — California, New York, Texas, Massachusetts, and 30+ other states
- Federal vs. state R&D credit comparison and stacking on the same QREs
- Funded research exclusions for contractors and government contract work
- VC-backed and equity-funded startup eligibility (equity is not "funded research")
- Pre-revenue startup payroll tax offset (§41(h), up to $500K/year, Form 8974)
- Audit and IDR readiness, contemporaneous documentation, and refund-claim requirements
